South House

Brooklyn · Est. NYC 2024

South House is a Brooklyn-based party series and creative collective that blends Southern hospitality with NYC's electric energy. We bring together music, fashion, and film people for immersive events that feel intimate, cinematic, and unmistakably ours.

Each gathering features live vocals, DJs, and collaborators from the South and beyond. We drop limited merch, build a community space, and invite guests to feel at home while dancing until sunrise.
