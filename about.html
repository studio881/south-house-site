About Us

South House is a premier lifestyle brand bridging the gap between Southern charm and New York City edge. Founded in 2024, we curate unforgettable experiences that celebrate community, culture, and creativity.

Our flagship events blend live music, art, and dining into immersive experiences that feel both intimate and electric. From rooftop sessions to full floor takeovers, every event is designed to leave an impression.
